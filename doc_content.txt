Critical integration rulesThese rules are especially important for a four-member Local AI project.Never pass raw camera video to the feedback LLM. Member 3 should extract relevant landmarks and measurements first.Record first and analyze after stopping, at least in the initial version. This greatly reduces the difficulty of running several AI models simultaneously.Use one shared session ID. Audio metrics, pose metrics, documents, feedback and stars must all reference the same practice session.Never calculate stars from the LLM's opinion. Use transparent numerical rules based on comparable sessions and declared targets.Ensure the complete app works offline after installation and model provisioning. Bundled models or an explicit one-time setup are needed; a hidden internet download during the demo is not acceptable.Allow partial results. If the camera fails but the microphone works, provide speech feedback rather than failing the entire session.Protect personal data. Do not automatically retain raw audio/video indefinitely. Give students clear deletion controls.9. Minimum acceptance criteriaBefore you consider the app finished, test these user journeys.Project completion checklist0/8First launchStudent creates a local profile, selects a language and reaches the dashboard without internet.Practice recordingStudent starts and stops a speech; audio recording and optional camera processing work.Speech analysisOffline recognition produces a transcript, and the app calculates measurable delivery metrics.Body analysisThe app reports supported visible movement measurements or explains why they are unavailable.FeedbackRecommendations correspond to actual analysis evidence, with no invented measurements.ProgressCompleted sessions are saved, and comparable repeated sessions can earn stars.Data privacyStudent can edit the profile and delete personal records and recordings.Offline verificationAll essential functionality works on the target device in airplane mode.Copy checklist10. Final scope recommendationFor the first stable version, your team should focus on this sequence:Student opens HAWKABUILDPractices a speech with microphone and optional cameraLocal AI processes speech and pose dataApp explains measurable strengths and areas for improvementStudent repeats the practice and tracks progressPDF support should initially mean extracting readable content and showing which prepared speech sections may have been omitted. Accurate evaluation of whether the student understood all concepts is a separate, harder feature. Defer unrestricted semantic evaluation until it can be tested.Similarly, the local LLM is an enhancement to the feedback engine, not a prerequisite for a functioning MVP. Your app can still provide valid, useful feedback using local speech recognition, landmark detection, and explainable rules.The single most important milestone for your team is this: one student completes a speech, receives accurate measurable feedback, and sees the session saved—all while the phone is offline.Once that works reliably, add the cartoon character, document support, more detailed AI coaching, and visual polish. That gives HAWKABUILD the best chance of becoming a finished, defensible hackathon submission rather than an impressive collection of disconnected features.
